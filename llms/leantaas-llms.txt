# LeanTaaS

> LeanTaaS is a healthcare capacity-management software company founded in 2010 and headquartered in Santa Clara, California. Its iQueue product line applies lean principles and predictive/prescriptive machine learning to hospital operations, helping health systems raise utilization of the assets they already own — operating rooms, infusion chairs and inpatient beds — rather than build new capacity.

LeanTaaS does not operate a public API program. There is no developer portal,
no published API reference, no OpenAPI or AsyncAPI description, no SDKs and no
public sandbox. iQueue is sold and deployed as a contracted SaaS engagement with
hospitals and health systems; data integration with EHRs is arranged per
customer and is not publicly documented. Agents should not expect a
self-service integration path.

## Products

- [iQueue for Operating Rooms](https://leantaas.com/products/): Surgical block utilization and OR access — releasing unused block time and matching it to demand.
- [iQueue for Infusion Centers](https://leantaas.com/products/): Infusion chair scheduling and patient flow optimization.
- [iQueue for Inpatient Flow](https://leantaas.com/products/): Bed capacity management and workforce alignment.
- [iQueue Autopilot](https://leantaas.com/products/): Generative-AI layer over the iQueue product line, introduced 2023.
- [Transformation Services](https://leantaas.com/products/): Implementation and change-management services accompanying the software.
